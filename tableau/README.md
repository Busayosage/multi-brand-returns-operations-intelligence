# Tableau Public showcase

**[Open Multi-Brand Returns Operations Intelligence](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/oluwasbusayomi.oseola/viz/Returns_Intelligence_20261019_Checked/RetailPerformance_)**

Published and visually reviewed on 19 October 2026. Use the six dashboard tabs to explore:

1. Retail Performance — January–November 2010 versus 2011, GBP, four KPIs and monthly trend.
2. Country Performance — country-level net merchandise value changes.
3. Customer Concentration — Australia and EIRE customer contributions; concentration guides investigation, not causal conclusions.
4. Cancellation Analysis — product concentration and the equal-and-opposite £77,183.60 sale/cancellation pair recorded 16 minutes apart.
5. Synthetic Logistics — 5,000 fictional cases demonstrating SLA and stage-time monitoring, separate from observed retail transactions.
6. Improvement Roadmap — proposed review, reason-code capture, reconciliation and measured-pilot controls.

The tabs may appear in a different order in the published workbook.

## Interpretation

- Cancellation-marked records do not establish physical returns or their causes.
- Keep the 2011 headline cancellation-value ratio at **3.23%**. **2.38%** is a labelled sensitivity test excluding both entries of the reviewed pair.
- Synthetic carrier and warehouse differences reflect generator assumptions; they are not observed operational performance.
- Proposed controls are not measured improvements or realised savings.
- Net merchandise value is not profit. AOV uses eligible gross merchandise value divided by eligible sales orders.
